Key facts (checked November 2026):
- Bluetooth Classic uses the 2.4 GHz band, 2402-2480 MHz, split into 79 channels
- It hops to a new channel 1,600 times a second (every 625 microseconds)
- Adaptive frequency hopping avoids busy channels; at least 20 channels stay in use
- One leader device can talk to up to 7 active devices in a piconet
- Class 1 reaches about 100 m (100 mW), Class 2 about 10 m (2.5 mW), Class 3 about 1 m (1 mW)
- Bluetooth Low Energy uses 40 channels, 3 of them for advertising
