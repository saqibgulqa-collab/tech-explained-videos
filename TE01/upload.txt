Key figures (checked November 2026):
- 607 cable systems in service and 102 planned (TeleGeography, 2026)
- About 1.7 million km of subsea cable; 150-200 incidents a year, about three quarters from fishing gear and anchors (International Cable Protection Committee via The National, Nov 2025)
- Deep-sea cables are 17-20 mm wide; armoured cables up to ~50 mm (ICPC)
- MAREA: 6,600 km, Virginia Beach to Bilbao, up to 160 Tbps at launch
- 1858 cable: Queen Victoria's 98-word message took almost 16 hours (IEEE Spectrum)
