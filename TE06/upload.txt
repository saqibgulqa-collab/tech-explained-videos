Key figures (checked November 2026):
- iPhone 14 and earlier: about 80% capacity after 500 full cycles; iPhone 15 and later: 1,000 cycles, under ideal conditions (MacRumors, Apple)
- Typical users: roughly 365-400 cycles a year (SlashGear)
- Capacity after 1 year of storage: 25 C at 40% charge about 96%, at 100% about 80%; 40 C at 100% about 65% (Battery University BU-808)
- Every 0.10 V lower peak charge roughly doubles cycle life; shallow cycles last far longer than deep ones (Battery University BU-808)
- Lithium trapped in the SEI is the main cause of capacity fade (The Electrochemical Society)
