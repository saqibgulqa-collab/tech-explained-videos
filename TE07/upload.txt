Key facts (checked November 2026):
- ANC uses a microphone and an out-of-phase wave to cancel noise by destructive interference; works best on constant low-frequency sound, less on voices and sudden sounds; high frequencies are handled by passive isolation (Wikipedia: Noise-cancelling headphones)
- Feedforward (outside mic), feedback (inside mic) and hybrid designs (Wikipedia)
- History: Paul Lueg patent 1936; Amar Bose's idea on a flight in 1978; Rutan and Yeager wore prototype Bose headsets on the 1986 non-stop round-the-world flight; Bose consumer QuietComfort from 2000 (Wikipedia)
- Some people feel dizziness with strong ANC; lowering ANC strength can help (SoundGuys)
- Wavelengths calculated with the speed of sound at about 343 m/s
