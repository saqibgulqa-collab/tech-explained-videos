Key figures (checked November 2026):
- 32 operational GPS satellites as of October 2026 (US Coast Guard NAVCEN status via KeepTrack); US commits to at least 24 available
- Orbit about 20,200 km, 6 orbital planes, two orbits a day (Gunter's Space Page)
- Relativity: about -7 us/day (speed) and +45 us/day (weaker gravity), net +38 us/day; uncorrected about 10 km error a day (Tech for Space / ESA material)
- GPS-enabled smartphones: typically within 4.9 m (16 ft) under open sky (GPS.gov)
- Navigation message at 50 bit/s; unassisted start can take up to 12.5 minutes (Assisted GNSS)
