Key figures (checked November 2026):
- Bayer filter: 50% green, 25% red, 25% blue; Bryce E. Bayer, Eastman Kodak, US patent 3,971,065 (1976)
- Demosaicing estimates about two thirds of the colour data
- Samsung ISOCELL HP3: 200 MP, 0.56 um pixels, 1/1.4-inch; 4-to-1 binning = 50 MP (1.12 um), 16-to-1 = 12.5 MP (2.24 um) (PetaPixel)
- Google HDR+ buffers the latest 9 or 15 frames; Night Sight (2018) captures 6-15 frames, up to 1 s each when still, 1/15 s or less handheld (SlashGear on Google's research)
